Weekly Schedule

For school 8 AM–3:30 PM. ~5.5 hrs study/day outside school. Every day rotates so no subject goes >2 days without a touch.

Weekday (Mon–Fri)

6:00–7:00 AMMaths — new topic or 10 PYQs (freshest mind = hardest subject)
8 AM–3:30 PMSchool — Class 12 chapters taught here = free JEE prep. Stay alert.
4:00–5:00 PMRest — mandatory. Skipping this kills the evening session.
5:00–6:30 PMPhysics or Chemistry — rotate daily. New concept + 10 PYQs same session.
6:30–7:30 PMMaths problems — practice on the morning topic
7:30–8:15 PMDinner
8:15–9:45 PMThird subject — whichever wasn't covered in 5–6:30 slot
9:45–10:15 PMFormula review + Error Log — 15 min max. Sleep by 10:30 PM.

Weekend (Sat–Sun)

Sat AMDeep work — hardest pending chapter or a chapter revision session
Sat PMSubject mini-mock (25 Qs, 45 min, timed) on this week's chapters
Sun AMFull 3-hr mock from October onwards — phone off, no breaks, real conditions
Sun PMMock review — every wrong answer diagnosed. Plan next week.

How to use each chapter
  • After theory: do the chapter's PYQ target before calling it "done".
  • Topic Test: use a chapter-wise test from your test series or NTA/official practice material. Record the score here.
  • Revision sheet: one page only — formulas/reactions, traps, 3–5 starred questions and your personal mistakes.
  • Retest: repeat after 7–14 days. A chapter is strong when you can solve without reopening theory.
Recommended external practice

Use National Test Abhyas for NTA-style mock practice and the official JEE Main site for official notices, syllabus and released papers. For chapter-wise PYQs, use the PYQ book/test series you already own rather than collecting multiple sources.

Best Resources

Ranked by JEE Main relevance. The rule: go deep on fewer sources, not shallow on many.

Physics

📚 Books
  • HC Verma Vol 1 & 2 — Concepts of Physics. Start here. Do ALL worked examples + exercises.
  • DC Pandey — After HC Verma per chapter, use DC Pandey for extra problem practice. Don't read theory from here.
  • NCERT Physics XI & XII — Read fully. NCERT examples appear in JEE papers directly.
▶ Free Video Lectures
  • Physics Wallah (Alakh Pandey) on YouTube — best free JEE Physics. Search "Alakh Pandey [chapter] JEE".
  • Namo Kaul (Unacademy) — Optics, Electrostatics. Very exam-focused.
  • Vedantu JEE — Good for revision lectures and PYQ sessions.

Chemistry

📚 Books
  • NCERT Chemistry XI & XII — PRIMARY source. Read line by line. Inorganic questions are almost lifted from NCERT.
  • OP Tandon — Physical Chemistry — For Physical Chemistry numericals. Better depth than NCERT.
  • MS Chauhan — Organic Chemistry — Best named-reactions and mechanisms guide for JEE.
▶ Free Video Lectures
  • Physics Wallah (Prashant Jain) — Excellent Organic Chemistry series on YouTube.
  • Paaras Thakur (PW) — Physical Chemistry, very JEE-aligned.
  • AJ Sir (Vedantu) — Inorganic Chemistry. Fast, dense, perfect for revision.

Mathematics

📚 Books
  • RD Sharma XI & XII — Start here if basics are weak. Do all examples first.
  • Cengage Maths (G. Tewani) — Gold standard for JEE Maths problem practice. Graded difficulty.
  • Arihant Skills in Mathematics — Coordinate Geometry and Algebra deep dives.
▶ Free Video Lectures
  • Mathongo (Aman Dhattarwal) — Best free JEE Maths on YouTube. Calculus & Algebra especially strong.
  • Mohit Tyagi — Deep, concept-first. Excellent for Calculus.
  • NJ Sir (Unacademy) — Coordinate Geometry and Vectors.

Mock Tests & PYQ Platforms

  • NTA Practice Centre (nta.ac.in) — Official free mock interface. Closest to real exam.
  • PW JEE Test Series (pw.live) — Very affordable paid mocks, good quality.
  • Disha / MTG 41 Years Chapterwise PYQ books — essential. Do chapter-wise before full mocks.
  • Embibe / Testbook — Free limited mocks available for supplement.

One last honest note
5 months with a full Class 11 backlog is hard, not impossible. The students who fail this challenge waste the first month planning instead of doing. Settings & Data

Manage your progress data. Everything is stored in your browser — it doesn't go to any server.

📦 What is Export / Import?

Your ticked chapters, subtopics, phase tasks, and error log entries are all saved in your browser's localStorage — a small private storage built into every browser, tied to this device and this browser only.

Export downloads all your progress as a .json backup file to your device. Think of it like a save file.

Import loads a previously exported file and restores all your progress. Use this when you switch devices (phone ↔ laptop) or browsers.

Important: If you clear your browser's site data or cache, your progress is erased. Export a backup regularly — especially before clearing your browser.
